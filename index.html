About Fresh Farm Produce

Fresh Farm Produce connects local farmers here in Ghana directly with buyers. For farm pick-ups, pick a time to collect, check the produce, then pay.
Never send money before you see it.
Fresh Farm Produce also handles local deliveries within the same town, and courier delivery anywhere in the country.
